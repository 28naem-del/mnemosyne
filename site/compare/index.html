Mnemosyne Recall

Find more evidence.
Carry more forward.

Retrieval quality matters twice: when memories are selected, and when they must fit into the next turn.

Relevance within your scope

Default local scoring accounts for rare terms, repetition and document length using only authorized, time-eligible records under the query’s trust settings. Optional semantic retrieval searches the full indexed scope, then fusion and optional reranking select candidates under explicit limits.

Optional intelligence, on device

Mnemosyne On-Device Intelligence runs embeddings and reranking on your CPU. Provision runtime and model weights explicitly, then use their cache offline. No model is needed for the local store or default lexical search.

Mnemosyne Timeline

A past you can trace.
A present you can check.

Separate when a fact held in the world from when the system learned it. Carry that distinction through recall and compiled context.

Change with an effective date

An evidenced correction preserves relevant earlier guidance until its effective boundary, then withholds obsolete descendants. Recorded correction causes support historical reads; ambiguous legacy invalidations remain withheld. This is conservative source-level retirement, not yet selective repair of every independent claim.

Freshness before reuse

Explicit source checks distinguish confirmed, changed and unavailable evidence. Action read sets revalidate declared local dependencies immediately before your application considers the action.

Propose, then test

Candidate skills accumulate controller-run trials before becoming ordinary advice. The recommended policy requires at least two distinct tasks and two distinct verifiers; the compatibility default remains one of each. Mnemosyne Migration

Move forward.
Keep your history.

Start beside the memory system your agents already use, and increase adoption as evidence accumulates.

Begin with coexistence

Connect a read-only adapter to existing search. Copy records your agents retrieve, then use policy-gated paired recall checks to move from shadow to assist to local preference. The old store remains checked on every query and is never changed by the bridge.

Keep control of the transition

Return routing to the existing system when needed. Choose a reviewed full export import, inspect source bytes, undo unchanged batches and rehearse whole-database recovery. Progress reports cover the queries observed so far.

Evidence before claims

Progress you can
put a number on.

A frozen, matched 500-question corpus measures our recall upgrade against our earlier scorer. It does not establish a ranking against other products.

90.43% complete retrieval coverage

All annotated source sessions were retrieved for 425 of 470 answerable questions, up from 386 (82.13%) with the earlier scorer. Both conditions use the same corpus and top-20 chunk limit; all 500 questions were processed.

81.28% complete packed coverage

Under the same 8,192-byte context budget, complete source-session coverage rises from 367 to 382 of 470 answerable questions. Stronger scoring adds CPU work; the protocol reports retrieval timing alongside coverage.

What those numbers establish

These are session-level evidence scores, not generated-answer accuracy. A returned chunk can earn source-session credit without containing the answer sentence. Same-day-compatible timing permits later sources on the question’s calendar day, so this is not a strict no-future-evidence experiment.

Benefit and failure stay visible

The separate agent harness compares no memory, recent history, lexical memory and adaptive context using a matched reader and budgets. It can track answer quality, grounding, stale actions and negative transfer. Scripted examples check the mechanics; generated-answer quality still needs measurement with your reader and tasks.
